          <target>On the fifteenth day of the fourth month, in the second year of Jian Ning, the Emperor ascended the throne in the Hall of Warm Virtue. The Emperor, startled, fell back, and his attendants hurriedly carried him into the palace, while the officials fled in all directions. In a moment, the serpent vanished. In the first year of Guang He, hens turned into roosters. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor over ten zhang long flew into the Hall of Warm Virtue. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow appeared at the Jade Hall; the cliffs of Wuyuan all collapsed and split apart.
