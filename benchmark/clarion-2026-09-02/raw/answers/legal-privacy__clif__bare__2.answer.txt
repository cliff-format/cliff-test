<paper-title>

target: "大规模审计同意记录：三个同意门户的实地研究"
type: sentence
status: translated

<paper-abstract-opening>
source: "Consent records are the evidence a company relies on when a regulator asks why a person's data was used, yet the records themselves are rarely audited. We report a field study of three consent portals run by mid-sized companies over nine months."
target: "同意记录是公司在监管机构询问为何使用某人的数据时所依赖的证据，然而这些记录本身却很少被审计。我们报告了一项对三家中型公司运营的同意门户进行的为期九个月的实地研究。"
type: sentence
status: translated

<paper-abstract-method>
source: "Each portal was exercised with a fixed script of routine requests, and every receipt it returned was compared against the entry that the portal had written to its own log."
target: "每个门户均使用固定的常规请求脚本进行测试，并将其返回的每份回执与门户写入自身日志的条目进行比较。"
type: sentence
status: translated

<paper-abstract-findings>
source: "Two of the three portals lost the timestamp of at least one withdrawal, and none of them could show, without manual work, which version of the notice a person had agreed to. We argue that a consent record is worth no more than the audit trail behind it, and we publish the test script so that other teams can repeat the study."
target: "三个门户中有两个丢失了至少一项撤回的时间戳，并且没有一个门户能够在无需人工操作的情况下显示某人同意的是哪个版本的通知。我们认为，同意记录的价值不超过其背后的审计追踪，并且我们公开测试脚本，以便其他团队能够重复该研究。"
type: sentence
status: translated
