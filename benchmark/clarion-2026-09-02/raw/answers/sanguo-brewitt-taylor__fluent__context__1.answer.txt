chapter-1-001 = On the fifteenth day of the fourth month in the second year of Jianning, the Emperor held court in the Hall of Warm Virtue. He had just taken his seat when a violent wind arose in the corner of the hall, and a great green serpent came flying down from the beams, coiling itself upon the throne. The Emperor fell back in fright, and his attendants hurried him into the palace, while all the officials fled in confusion. In a moment the serpent vanished. Suddenly there came a great clap of thunder and a downpour of rain, with hail as well, which did not cease until midnight, destroying countless houses. In the second month of the fourth year of Jianning, there was an earthquake in Luoyang; and the sea waters overflowed, sweeping all the coastal inhabitants into the waves. In the first year of Guanghe, hens turned into roosters. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor more than ten zhang long flew into the Hall of Warm Virtue. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow appeared at the Jade Hall; and the cliffs of Wuyuan all collapsed. These ill omens were many and various, not of one kind alone.
