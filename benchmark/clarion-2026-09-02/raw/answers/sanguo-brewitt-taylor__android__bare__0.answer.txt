    <string name="chapter-1-001">On the fifteenth day of the fourth month in the second year of Jianning, the Emperor held court in the Hall of Warm Virtue. As he ascended the throne, a violent wind suddenly rose from the corner of the hall, and a great green serpent flew down from the beams, coiling upon the imperial seat. The Emperor fell back in fright, and his attendants hurriedly carried him into the palace, while all the officials fled in panic. In a moment, the serpent vanished. Suddenly there came great thunder and rain, along with hail, which continued until midnight, destroying countless houses. In the second month of the fourth year of Jianning, an earthquake struck Luoyang; and the sea overflowed its bounds, sweeping the coastal inhabitants into the waves. In the first year of Guanghe, hens turned into roosters. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor over ten zhang long flew into the Hall of Warm Virtue. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow appeared in the Jade Hall; and the cliffs of Wuyuan all collapsed. These portents of ill omen were many and varied.</string>

The Emperor also favored Lady Wang, who bore the prince Xie. Empress He, in jealousy, poisoned Lady Wang. The prince Xie was raised in the palace of Grand Empress Dowager Dong. Grand Empress Dowager Dong was the mother of Emperor Ling and the wife of Liu Chang, Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion. Originally, because Emperor Huan had no sons, the son of the Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion was brought in to succeed, and that was Emperor Ling. When Emperor Ling inherited the throne, he brought his mother into the palace and honored her as Grand Empress Dowager.</string>

    <string name="chapter-2-003">The next day, the Empress Dowager ordered He Jin to participate in the affairs of the Secretariat, and the rest were all given offices. Grand Empress Dowager Dong summoned Zhang Rang and others to the palace to discuss, saying, "He Jin\'s sister—I was the one who first promoted her. Now her child sits on the imperial throne, and all the officials, inside and out, are her confidants. Her power is too great. What shall I do?" Rang memorialized, saying, "Your Majesty may hold court behind a curtain and rule from behind it; enfeoff the prince Xie as a king; give the imperial uncle Dong Zhong a high office with military authority; and favor us with important positions—then great deeds can be accomplished."</string>

    <string name="chapter-2-004">Grand Empress Dowager Dong was greatly pleased. The next day at court, she issued a decree enfeoffing the prince Xie as King of Chenliu, Dong Zhong as General of Agile Cavalry, and Zhang Rang and others to participate in court affairs. Empress Dowager He, seeing Grand Empress Dowager Dong monopolizing power, held a banquet in the palace and invited Grand Empress Dowager Dong to attend. When the wine was half finished, Empress Dowager He rose, raised her cup, bowed twice, and said, "We are all women; participating in court affairs is not fitting for us. In former times, Empress Lü, because she held great power, saw her entire clan of a thousand members slaughtered. Now we should remain deep within the palace; let the great ministers and elders deliberate on matters of state themselves—this would be the fortune of the nation. I beg you to heed this." Grand Empress Dowager Dong flew into a rage and said, "You poisoned Lady Wang out of jealousy. Now, relying on your son being emperor and your brother He Jin\'s power, you dare to speak recklessly! I can order the Agile Cavalry to cut off your brother\'s head as easily as turning my hand!" Empress Dowager He also grew angry and said, "I advised you with kind words—why do you turn angry instead?" Empress Dowager Dong said, "Your family are but butchers and wine-sellers—what understanding do you have?"</string>

    <string name="chapter-2-005">A moment later, He Jin went in to inform the Empress Dowager of his wish to execute the eunuchs. Empress Dowager He said, "The eunuchs have commanded the palace since the founding of Han—this is the established custom of our dynasty. The late emperor has only just departed this world; if you wish to slay the old ministers, you would not be honoring the ancestral temple." Jin was by nature an indecisive man, and hearing the Empress Dowager\'s words, he assented vaguely and withdrew. Yuan Shao met him and asked, "How goes the great matter?" Jin said, "The Empress Dowager will not consent. What can be done?" Shao said, "You may summon the heroes of the four quarters, bring their troops to the capital, and exterminate all the eunuchs. At this critical moment, the Empress Dowager will have no choice but to comply." Jin said, "This plan is excellent!" He then issued proclamations to all the provinces, summoning them to the capital.</string>

    <string name="chapter-2-006">The chief clerk Chen Lin said, "This cannot be! As the saying goes, \'Covering one\'s eyes to catch sparrows\' is self-deception. Even for trifling matters one cannot deceive oneself into success, let alone for the great affairs of state! Now you, General, rely on the imperial majesty, hold the key to military power, and stride like a dragon and pace like a tiger, with all decisions in your heart: if you wish to execute the eunuchs, it is like blowing on a great furnace to singe a hair. You need only act swiftly, exercise your authority, and decide at once, and heaven and man will follow you. But instead you issue proclamations to outside ministers, bringing them to the capital—heroes will gather, each with his own designs. This is what is called holding a spear by the blade and handing the handle to another; success will not be achieved, and chaos will arise instead." He Jin laughed and said, "This is the view of a coward!" A man nearby clapped his hands and laughed loudly, saying, "This matter is as easy as turning over one\'s hand—why so much debate!" Looking at him, it was Cao Cao. Indeed: To rid the ruler of the petty men beside him, one must heed the counsel of the wise in court. What words Cao Cao spoke, listen to the next chapter to learn.</string>
