<abstract>
source: "Financial audit trails grow by millions of events each day, and the teams that review them need detectors that flag genuine anomalies without overwhelming human analysts. We evaluate three detection approaches on a corpus of synthetic ledger events, measuring false-positive rate, end-to-end latency and processing throughput under adversarial noise. Our results show that a sequence model built on learned event Embedding improves robustness to noise while keeping latency below the interactive threshold. We release the evaluation harness and the synthetic corpus to support reproducible comparison."
target: "金融审计追踪每天新增数百万条事件，负责审查这些数据的团队需要能够标记真正异常且不会让人类分析师不堪重负的检测器。我们在一个合成账本事件语料库上评估了三种检测方法，测量了对抗性噪声下的误报率、端到端延迟和处理吞吐量。我们的结果表明，基于学习的事件 Embedding 构建的序列模型提高了对噪声的健壮性，同时将延迟保持在交互阈值以下。我们发布了评估工具和合成语料库，以支持可复现的比较。"
status: translated
context: "Full abstract; keep the evaluation framing and the three measurements intact."
