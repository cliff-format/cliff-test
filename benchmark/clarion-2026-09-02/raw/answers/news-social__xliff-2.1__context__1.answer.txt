        <mda:meta type="title">A city social feed the day a tram line opens: posts, replies, notifications and comments</mda:meta>
        <mda:meta type="info">One day of social media from the invented city of Sablewick, where a new tram line called the Riverside Loop has just opened. Audience: ordinary users of a public social platform and readers commenting under a local news site. The material is posts, replies to posts, in-app notifications and reader comments, written in a casual present-day voice; handles, hashtags, emoji and message arguments are part of the text and belong to the platform, not to the sentence.</mda:meta>
        <mda:meta type="standard">zh-CN house style: one space between Han text and Latin letters or digits, fullwidth punctuation inside Han sentences, no textbook jargon. Keep @ handles, hashtags and emoji exactly as the source writes them, in the same position. Render current internet slang with an equally current Chinese expression instead of word for word. Keep the offhand voice of a post and do not lift it into news register. Render the emotion tag in the wording, the particles and the punctuation, never as a neutral gloss. Preserve ICU arguments, their names and their structure. A line name, place or account that appears more than once must read the same way in every entry of this file, and a reply must be completed from the message it answers.</mda:meta>
