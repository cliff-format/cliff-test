        <mda:meta type="title">Minimal-pair diagnostic set where the brief is decisive</mda:meta>
        <mda:meta type="info">The probe stratum measures what a translation brief is worth: entries come in minimal pairs whose source text is identical or nearly so, and only one CLIF field (emotion, type, context or max-width) tells the two members apart. A translation that ignores that field must fail at least one member. Every item is original text written for CLARION, drawn from generic software, transit, stage, warehouse and support scenarios.</mda:meta>
        <mda:meta type="standard">Use one space between Han text and Latin letters or digits, and fullwidth punctuation inside Han sentences. Render the emotion tag in the wording, particles and punctuation, never as a neutral gloss. Honor the type field's register: a label stays a bare noun, a sentence is a full instruction, an idiom is an idiomatic wish, a proper noun is a place name. Choose the referent the context line names. Fit the declared max-width in display cells, where Latin letters and digits count one cell and Han or fullwidth characters count two. No textbook jargon.</mda:meta>
