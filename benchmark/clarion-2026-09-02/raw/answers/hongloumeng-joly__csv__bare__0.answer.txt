Though I dare not say they surpass all the persons in the books of former ages, yet the events and their causes and consequences can dispel sorrow and relieve boredom, and there are a few crooked poems and familiar sayings that can provoke laughter over food and wine. The people of today—the poor are burdened daily by food and clothing, and the rich harbor hearts of discontent. Even if they have a moment of leisure, there are matters of greed, lust, love of goods, and seeking after worries—where would they find the time to read books of governance and morality? Therefore, in this story of mine, I do not wish the world to marvel at its wonders, nor do I insist that the world delight in reading it.
