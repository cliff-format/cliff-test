    target: In the sixth year of Zhongping, in the fourth month of summer, Emperor Ling fell gravely ill and summoned the Grand General He Jin to the palace to discuss matters of succession. That He Jin had risen from a butcher's family; because his sister entered the palace as a Noble Lady and bore the prince Bian, she was established as Empress, and Jin thereby gained weighty power and office. The Emperor also favored Lady Wang, who bore the prince Xie. Empress He, in jealousy, poisoned Lady Wang to death. The prince Xie was raised in the palace of Empress Dowager Dong. Empress Dowager Dong was the mother of Emperor Ling and the wife of Liu Chang, Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion. Originally, because Emperor Huan had no sons, the son of the Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion was brought in and established—this was Emperor Ling. When Emperor Ling succeeded to the great inheritance, he brought his mother into the palace to be cared for and honored her as Empress Dowager.

The late emperor has only recently left the world; if you wish to execute the old ministers, that is no way to honor the ancestral temple." Jin said: "The Empress Dowager will not consent. What can be done?"
