      month, the Emperor held court in the Hall of Warm Virtue. As he had just taken
      his seat, a fierce wind suddenly rose in the corner of the hall, and a great
      green serpent was seen descending from the beams, coiling itself upon the
      throne. The Emperor fell back in fright, and his attendants hurriedly carried
      him into the palace, while all the officials fled in confusion. In a moment
      the serpent vanished. Then suddenly there came a great thunderstorm with hail,
      which lasted until midnight and destroyed countless houses. In the second
      month of the fourth year of Chien-ning, there was an earthquake in Loyang;
      and again the sea overflowed its bounds, and the dwellers along the coast were
      all swept away by the great waves. In the first year of Kuang-ho, hens were
      transformed into cocks. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor
      more than ten chang in length flew into the Hall of Warm Virtue. In the seventh
      month of autumn, a rainbow appeared in the Jade Hall; and the cliffs of the
      Wuyuan mountains all crumbled and split apart. These various portents of ill
      omen were not of one kind alone.

      named Chang Chio, one Chang Pao, and one Chang Liang. Chang Chio had originally
      been a scholar who had failed in the examinations. Once, while gathering herbs
      in the mountains, he met an old man with blue eyes and a youthful face, who
      carried a staff of wild pear wood. The old man called Chio into a cave and
      gave him three volumes of a heavenly book, saying: "This is called the Essential
      Art of Great Peace. When you have obtained it, you should proclaim the transforming
      influence of Heaven on its behalf and save all the people of the world. If
      you harbor any disloyal thought, you will surely receive an evil retribution."
      Chio bowed and asked his name. The old man said: "I am the Old Immortal of
      Nan-hua." Having spoken, he turned into a puff of clear wind and departed.
