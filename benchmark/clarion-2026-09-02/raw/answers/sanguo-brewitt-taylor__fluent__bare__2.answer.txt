Now her child has ascended the imperial throne, and all the ministers inside and outside are his confidants. Her power and authority are too great. What shall I do?" Rang memorialized: "Your Majesty may hold court and govern from behind the curtain; enfeoff Prince Xie as a king; promote the Imperial Uncle Dong Zhong to high office with command of the military; and give heavy responsibility to us your servants. Then great things may be accomplished."

# clif:source = Empress Dowager Dong was greatly pleased. The next day she held court and issued an edict, enfeoffing Prince Xie as Prince of Chenliu, Dong Zhong as General of Flying Cavalry, and allowing Zhang Rang and others to participate in court government. Empress Dowager He, seeing Empress Dowager Dong monopolizing power, held a banquet in the palace and invited Empress Dowager Dong to attend. When the wine was half finished, Empress Dowager He rose, raised her cup, bowed twice, and said: "We are both women; participating in court government is not proper for us. In former times, because Empress Lü held great power, a thousand members of her clan were put to death. Now we should remain deep within the palace; let the great ministers and elder statesmen deliberate on matters of state themselves. This would be the fortune of the nation. I beg you to heed this." Empress Dowager Dong flew into a rage and said: "You poisoned Lady Wang to death out of jealousy. Now, relying on your son being emperor and your brother He Jin's power, you dare to speak recklessly! I could order the Flying Cavalry General to cut off your brother's head as easily as turning over my hand!" Empress Dowager He also angrily replied: "I advised you with good words; why do you respond with anger?" Empress Dowager Dong said: "Your family are but butchers and peddlers of low birth—what understanding do you have!"
