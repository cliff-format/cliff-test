This file is the quest line 'The Ninth Light': journal briefs, objective lines, success and failure lines, item descriptions and achievement names. Audience: players on PC and handheld consoles. Transliterate the quartermaster's name 'Marrow' as 马洛; never translate it as 骨髓 and never spell it 马罗. Render 'Ninth Light' identically in every entry that mentions it. Keep engine placeholders such as {playerName}, ICU MessageFormat structure and key hints such as [E] exactly as the source writes them. Use one space between Han text and Latin letters or digits, and fullwidth punctuation inside Han sentences. Avoid textbook jargon: write 稳健 rather than 鲁棒, 默认 rather than 缺省, 分辨率 rather than 解析度.
