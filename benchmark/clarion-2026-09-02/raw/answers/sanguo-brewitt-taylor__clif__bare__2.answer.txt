target: "In the sixth year of Zhongping, in the fourth month of summer, Emperor Ling was gravely ill and summoned the Grand General He Jin to the palace to discuss matters of succession. That He Jin had risen from a butcher's family; because his sister had entered the palace as a noble lady and given birth to Prince Bian, she was established as Empress, and Jin thereby gained weighty power and office. The Emperor also favored Lady Wang, who gave birth to Prince Xie. Empress He, jealous, poisoned Lady Wang to death. Prince Xie was raised in the palace of Empress Dowager Dong. Empress Dowager Dong was the mother of Emperor Ling and the wife of Liu Chang, Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion. Originally, because Emperor Huan had no sons, the son of the Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion was brought in and established, and this was Emperor Ling. When Emperor Ling succeeded to the great inheritance, he brought his mother to be raised in the palace and honored her as Empress Dowager."

<chapter-2-004>

target: "Empress Dowager Dong was greatly pleased. The next day, holding court, Empress Dowager Dong issued an edict, enfeoffing Prince Xie as Prince of Chenliu, Dong Zhong as General of Agile Cavalry, and Zhang Rang and others to participate together in court government. Empress Dowager He, seeing Empress Dowager Dong monopolizing power, held a banquet in the palace and invited Empress Dowager Dong to attend. When the wine was half drunk, Empress Dowager He rose, holding a cup, and bowed twice, saying: \"We are all women; participating in court government is not fitting for us. In former times, Empress Lü, because she held great power, had her entire clan of a thousand mouths put to death. Now we should dwell deep within the palace; let the great affairs of the court be discussed by the senior ministers and elders themselves. This would be the fortune of the state. I beg you to heed this.\" Empress Dowager Dong angrily said: \"You poisoned Lady Wang to death, with a heart full of jealousy. Now, relying on your son being the ruler and your brother He Jin's power, you dare to speak recklessly! I could order the Agile Cavalry to cut off your brother's head as easily as turning over my hand!\" Empress He also angrily said: \"I advised you with good words; why do you instead turn angry?\" Empress Dowager Dong said: \"Your family are but butchers and wine-sellers of low birth; what understanding do you have!\""
