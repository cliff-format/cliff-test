The Emperor, startled, fell backward, and his attendants hurriedly carried him into the palace, while the assembled officials all fled in confusion. In a moment, the serpent disappeared. Then suddenly there came great thunder and heavy rain, with hail, which did not cease until midnight, destroying countless houses. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow appeared in the Jade Hall; and the cliffs of Wuyuan all crumbled and split. These various omens of misfortune were not limited to a single instance.</target>
