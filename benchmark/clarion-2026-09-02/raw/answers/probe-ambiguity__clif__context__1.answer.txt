A translation that ignores that field must fail at least one member. Every item is original text written for CLARION, drawn from generic software, transit, stage, warehouse and support scenarios."
standard: "Use one space between Han text and Latin letters or digits, and fullwidth punctuation inside Han sentences. Render the emotion tag in the wording, particles and punctuation, never as a neutral gloss. Honor the type field's register: a label stays a bare noun, a sentence is a full instruction, an idiom is an idiomatic wish, a proper noun is a place name. Choose the referent the context line names. Fit the declared max-width in display cells, where Latin letters and digits count one cell and Han or fullwidth characters count two. No textbook jargon."
