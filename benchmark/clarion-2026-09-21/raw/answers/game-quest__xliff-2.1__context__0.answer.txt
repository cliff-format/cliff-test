        <mda:meta type="info">Saltwake is a seafaring adventure game set in a drowned archipelago where couriers called signal runners carry lantern messages between lighthouses. This file is the quest line 'The Ninth Light': journal briefs, objective lines, success and failure lines, item descriptions and achievement names. Audience: players on PC and handheld consoles. Objective lines are shown twice, once on the wide journal page and once in the narrow on-screen tracker, and the system strings are rendered by the game engine.</mda:meta>
        <mda:meta type="standard">Translate the keeper's name 'Willow' semantically as 柳; never transliterate it as 薇洛, 威洛 or 维洛, and never turn it into the tree 柳树. Transliterate the quartermaster's name 'Marrow' as 马洛; never translate it as 骨髓 and never spell it 马罗. Render 'Ninth Light' identically in every entry that mentions it. Keep engine placeholders such as {playerName}, ICU MessageFormat structure and key hints such as [E] exactly as the source writes them. Use one space between Han text and Latin letters or digits, and fullwidth punctuation inside Han sentences. Avoid textbook jargon: write 稳健 rather than 鲁棒, 默认 rather than 缺省, 分辨率 rather than 解析度. Objective lines carry a display-width budget in cells, where Latin letters and digits count one cell and Han or fullwidth characters count two; the narrow version must be rewritten shorter, not truncated.</mda:meta>
