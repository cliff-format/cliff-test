# cliff:source = The Meridian Harbor Bridge stayed shut for a third straight night on 14 March as crews worked through heavy rain to replace three suspension cables snapped in last week's storm, forcing tens of thousands of commuters onto two temporary ferry shuttles that carry only a fraction of the usual traffic.
lede = 3月14日，子午港大桥连续第三晚保持封闭。施工人员冒着大雨更换上周风暴中崩断的三根悬索，迫使数万名通勤者改乘两班临时轮渡接驳船，而其运力仅为平日客流的一小部分。

# cliff:source = Meridian Harbor Authority officials said on Tuesday that reopening depends on a full safety review, and that 78% of the bridge's 214 suspension cables have passed inspection while the rest are still being tensioned and measured. The authority has already pushed back three earlier reopening estimates, and its director, Corin Ashwell, warned that any wind above 40 kilometres per hour would halt the crane work and push the schedule back again.
body-1 = 子午港务局官员周二表示，大桥能否重新开放取决于一次全面的安全审查；大桥214根悬索中已有78%通过检查，其余仍在进行张拉和测量。该局此前已三次推迟重新开放的预估时间，局长科林·阿什韦尔警告说，只要风速超过每小时40公里，起重机作业就得停下，工期也会再次推迟。

# cliff:source = The closure has rippled across the city's road network. Detour signs push drivers onto a single ring road that is already carrying double its design load, and tailbacks at peak hour now stretch for more than 12 kilometres along the north bank. Ferry staff added two extra crossings after 6 a.m., but queues at the east pier still wrapped around the block by mid-morning, with some commuters reporting waits of over an hour just to reach the ticket hall.
body-2 = 封桥的连锁反应已波及全市路网。绕行标志把司机们引向唯一一条环路，而这条环路的实际负荷已是设计承载能力的两倍；高峰时段的拥堵车流如今沿北岸绵延超过12公里。轮渡工作人员在早上6点后加开了两班，但到上午过半，东码头的队伍仍绕着街区排了一圈，一些通勤者称光是排到售票大厅就等了一个多小时。

# cliff:source = For decades the harbor ferries were a curiosity — a slow, scenic way to cross the water that most commuters forgot existed. Three closed nights have made them essential.
feature-lede = 几十年来，港口轮渡不过是件稀罕物——一种缓慢而风景优美的过江方式，大多数通勤者早已忘了它的存在。而封桥的三个夜晚，让它们变得不可或缺。

# cliff:source = The morning boat from the east pier is now the only reason many people reach the office before ten. Its crew of three knows the regulars by name and by order: the woman with the folding bicycle, the teacher who grades papers against the rail, the chef who rides across with a crate of sourdough. What began as an emergency measure has quietly become a small community, complete with its own running jokes and a captain who narrates the crossing like a tour guide.
feature-body-1 = 如今，对许多人来说，能在十点前赶到办公室，全靠在东码头搭乘的那班早班船。三名船员对常客的名字和登船次序都了然于胸：那位带着折叠自行车的女士、靠着栏杆批改作业的老师、抱着一箱酸面包过江的厨师。一项起初的应急措施，已悄然变成一个小社区，有自己反复讲的段子，还有一位像导游一样一路讲解的船长。

# cliff:source = Not everyone is nostalgic. Shopkeepers on the far side say the detour has cut their weekday trade by nearly a fifth, and the ferry's single fare of 2 euros adds up for anyone crossing twice a day. The authority has promised a rebate scheme, but the details are still being drafted, and a petition asking for a temporary footbridge has gathered eleven thousand signatures in three days.
feature-body-2 = 并非所有人都怀旧。对岸的店主说，绕行让他们工作日的生意下滑了近五分之一；而对每天往返两次的人来说，轮渡单程2欧元的票价也积少成多。港务局已承诺推出补贴方案，但细节仍在起草中；一份要求搭建临时人行桥的请愿书三天内已征集到一万一千个签名。
