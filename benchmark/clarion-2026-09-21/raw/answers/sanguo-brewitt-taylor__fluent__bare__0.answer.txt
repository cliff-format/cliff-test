The Empress Dowager Dong was the mother of Emperor Ling and the wife of Liu Chang, Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion. Originally, because Emperor Huan had no son, Liu Chang's son had been brought to court and made Emperor — this was Emperor Ling. When Emperor Ling came to the throne, he brought his mother into the palace and honored her as Empress Dowager.
