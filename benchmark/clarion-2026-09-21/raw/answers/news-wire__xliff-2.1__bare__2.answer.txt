      <unit id="lede">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>The Meridian Harbor Bridge stayed shut for a third straight night on 14 March as crews worked through heavy rain to replace three suspension cables snapped in last week's storm, forcing tens of thousands of commuters onto two temporary ferry shuttles that carry only a fraction of the usual traffic.</source>
          <target>3月14日，梅里迪安港大桥连续第三个夜晚保持关闭。施工人员冒雨更换在上周风暴中断裂的三根悬索，迫使数万名通勤者改乘两艘临时渡轮接驳船，而其运力仅为平日交通量的一小部分。</target>
        </segment>
      </unit>
      <unit id="body-1">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>Meridian Harbor Authority officials said on Tuesday that reopening depends on a full safety review, and that 78% of the bridge's 214 suspension cables have passed inspection while the rest are still being tensioned and measured. The authority has already pushed back three earlier reopening estimates, and its director, Corin Ashwell, warned that any wind above 40 kilometres per hour would halt the crane work and push the schedule back again.</source>
          <target>梅里迪安港务局官员周二表示，大桥能否重新开放取决于全面的安全审查；大桥214根悬索中，已有78%通过检查，其余仍在进行张拉和测量。港务局此前已三次推迟重新开放的预估时间，其局长科林·阿什韦尔警告称，只要风速超过每小时40公里，起重机作业就会停止，工期也将再次推迟。</target>
        </segment>
      </unit>
      <unit id="body-2">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>The closure has rippled across the city's road network. Detour signs push drivers onto a single ring road that is already carrying double its design load, and tailbacks at peak hour now stretch for more than 12 kilometres along the north bank. Ferry staff added two extra crossings after 6 a.m., but queues at the east pier still wrapped around the block by mid-morning, with some commuters reporting waits of over an hour just to reach the ticket hall.</source>
          <target>此次封闭的影响已波及全市道路网络。绕行标志将司机引向唯一一条环路，而这条环路目前的通行量已是设计负荷的两倍；高峰时段的车辆排队沿北岸绵延超过12公里。渡轮工作人员在早上6点后加开两个班次，但到上午过半时，东码头的队伍仍绕着街区排了一圈，一些通勤者称仅为了到售票大厅就等了一个多小时。</target>
        </segment>
      </unit>
      <unit id="quote">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>Meridian Harbor Authority director Corin Ashwell told reporters the bridge would reopen “only once every cable passes inspection — not a minute earlier.”</source>
          <target>梅里迪安港务局局长科林·阿什韦尔告诉记者，大桥“只有在每一根缆索都通过检查后才会重新开放——一分钟也不会提前。”</target>

      <unit id="feature-lede">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>For decades the harbor ferries were a curiosity — a slow, scenic way to cross the water that most commuters forgot existed. Three closed nights have made them essential.</source>
          <target>几十年来，港口渡轮一直是个稀罕物——一种缓慢而风景优美的渡水方式，多数通勤者几乎忘了它的存在。而大桥连续三个夜晚的封闭，让它们变得不可或缺。</target>
        </segment>
      </unit>
      <unit id="feature-body-1">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>The morning boat from the east pier is now the only reason many people reach the office before ten. Its crew of three knows the regulars by name and by order: the woman with the folding bicycle, the teacher who grades papers against the rail, the chef who rides across with a crate of sourdough. What began as an emergency measure has quietly become a small community, complete with its own running jokes and a captain who narrates the crossing like a tour guide.</source>
          <target>如今，许多人能在十点前到办公室，全靠东码头开出的早班船。三名船员能叫出常客的名字，也熟悉他们登船的次序：带折叠自行车的女士、靠着栏杆批改作业的老师、带着一箱酸面包过河的厨师。最初只是应急措施，如今却悄然成了一个小社区，有自己的老梗，还有一位像导游一样讲解航程的船长。</target>
        </segment>
      </unit>
      <unit id="feature-body-2">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>Not everyone is nostalgic. Shopkeepers on the far side say the detour has cut their weekday trade by nearly a fifth, and the ferry's single fare of 2 euros adds up for anyone crossing twice a day. The authority has promised a rebate scheme, but the details are still being drafted, and a petition asking for a temporary footbridge has gathered eleven thousand signatures in three days.</source>
          <target>并非人人都怀旧。对岸的店主说，绕行让他们工作日的生意减少了近五分之一；而对每天往返两次的人来说，渡轮单程2欧元的票价累积起来也不便宜。港务局已承诺推出返款方案，但细节仍在起草中；一份要求修建临时人行桥的请愿书三天内已征集到一万一千个签名。</target>
        </segment>
      </unit>
      <unit id="feature-quote">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>Captain Dea Prowse, at the helm of the morning shuttle, put it plainly: “Nobody planned for this. We just showed up and started carrying people.”</source>
          <target>掌舵早班接驳船的迪娅·普劳斯船长直言不讳：“没人为此做过规划。我们只是来了，然后就开始载人。”</target>
