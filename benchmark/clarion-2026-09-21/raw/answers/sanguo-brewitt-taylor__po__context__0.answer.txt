msgstr "After a short while, He Jin entered and informed the Empress Dowager, wishing to put the eunuchs to death. Empress Dowager He said, 'It has been the practice of the Han that eunuchs administer the palace. The late Emperor has just left the world, and you wish to kill his old servants—that is not to honor the ancestral temple.' He Jin was by nature a man of no decision; hearing the Empress Dowager's words, he assented and withdrew. Yuan Shao met him and asked, 'How went the great matter?' He Jin said, 'The Empress Dowager will not consent; what can be done?' Yuan Shao said, 'You may summon heroes from all quarters to lead troops to the capital and put all the eunuchs to death. The situation is urgent and will not permit the Empress Dowager to refuse.' He Jin said, 'This plan is excellent!'
