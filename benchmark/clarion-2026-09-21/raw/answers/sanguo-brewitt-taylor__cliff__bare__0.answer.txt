target: "Shortly after, He Jin went in and told the Empress Dowager that he wished to put the palace eunuchs to death. The Empress Dowager He said, 'It has been the custom of the Han that the eunuchs command the forbidden precincts of the palace. The late Emperor has but lately left the world, and would you slay his old servants? That is no way to honor the ancestral temple.' He Jin was by nature a man of no decision; at the Empress Dowager's words he assented meekly and went out. Yuan Shao met him and asked, 'How stands the great matter?' He Jin answered, 'The Empress Dowager will not consent; what is to be done?' Yuan Shao said, 'Summon the heroes of the four quarters and order them to bring their troops to the capital, that all the eunuchs may be put to death.
