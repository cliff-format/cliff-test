      On the fifteenth day of the fourth month of the second year of Chien Ning, the Emperor
      held court in the Hall of Temperate Virtue. He had but ascended the throne when a violent
      wind rose at a corner of the hall, and a great green serpent flew down from the beam and
      coiled itself upon his seat. The Emperor fell over in terror; his attendants hastily
      hurried him into the palace, and all the assembled officials fled and hid themselves.
      In a moment the serpent was gone. Then suddenly came a great thunderstorm, with hail,
      which did not cease until midnight, and destroyed countless houses. In the second month
      of the fourth year of Chien Ning there was an earthquake at Lo-yang; and again the sea
      overflowed, and the dwellers along the coast were all swept by the great waves into the
      sea. In the first year of Kuang Ho, hens turned into cocks. On the first day of the sixth
      month, a black vapor more than a hundred feet long flew into the Hall of Temperate Virtue.
      In the seventh month of autumn a rainbow appeared at the Jade Hall, and the cliffs of
      Wu-yuan Mountain all crumbled and split. These manifold portents of evil were not of one
      kind only.
