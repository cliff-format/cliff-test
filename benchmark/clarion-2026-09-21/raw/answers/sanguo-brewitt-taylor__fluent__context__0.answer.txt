chapter-1-001 = In the second year of Jianning, on the fifteenth day of the fourth month, the Emperor went in state to Wende Hall. Just as he ascended the throne, a violent wind suddenly rose from a corner of the hall, and a great blue serpent flew down from the beam and coiled upon the throne. The Emperor fell in terror; his attendants hurried him into the palace, and all the officials fled for their lives. In a moment the serpent vanished. Then suddenly came great thunder and rain, with hail, which did not stop until midnight, destroying countless houses. In the second month of the fourth year of Jianning, an earthquake struck Luoyang; the sea also overflowed, and the coastal people were all swept into the sea by the great waves. In the first year of Guanghe, a hen was transformed into a cock. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor more than ten zhang long flew into Wende Hall. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow appeared in the Jade Hall; the cliffs of Wuyuan Mountain all collapsed and split. These manifold omens of evil were not confined to one.

Xuande's grandfather was Liu Xiong, his father Liu Hong. Hong had been recommended as filial and incorrupt and had also served as an official, but died early. Southeast of his house stood a great mulberry tree more than five zhang tall; seen from afar, it was dense and rounded like a carriage canopy.
