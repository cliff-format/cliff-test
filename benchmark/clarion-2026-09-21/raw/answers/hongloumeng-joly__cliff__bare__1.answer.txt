The author says of himself that, having lived through a dream and an illusion, he concealed the truth and, borrowing the tale of the \"Stone of Spiritual Understanding,\" wrote this book, The Story of the Stone; hence the name \"Zhen Shiyin\" — Truth Concealed. But what events does the book record, and what persons? Shame I have in plenty, and remorse that is of no use — there is nothing to be done now.
