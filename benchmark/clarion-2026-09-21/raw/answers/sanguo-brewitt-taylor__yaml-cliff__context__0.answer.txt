      On the fifteenth day of the fourth month of the second year of Chien Ning, the
      Emperor held court in the Hall of Virtue. Just as he ascended the throne, a violent
      wind rose from a corner of the hall, and a great green serpent flew down from
      the beam and coiled upon the chair. The Emperor fell back in terror; his attendants
      rushed to his aid and carried him into the palace, while all the officials fled
      in panic. In a moment the serpent vanished. Then suddenly came great thunder and
      rain, with hail, which did not cease until midnight and destroyed countless houses.
      In the second month of the fourth year of Chien Ning, there was an earthquake
      at Loyang; the sea overflowed, and the people along the coast were all swept into
      the sea by great waves. In the first year of Kuang Ho, hens turned into roosters.
      On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapour more than ten cubits long
      flew into the Hall of Virtue. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow appeared
      in the Jade Hall; and the cliffs of Wu Yuan Mountain all crumbled and split. These
      various evil omens were not confined to one kind.

Now, General, you rely on the imperial majesty, hold the military power, and move like a dragon or tiger, with all things in your heart.
