    <group id="abstract">
      <unit id="abstract">
        <segment state="translated">
          <source>Financial audit trails grow by millions of events each day, and the teams that review them need detectors that flag genuine anomalies without overwhelming human analysts. We evaluate three detection approaches on a corpus of synthetic ledger events, measuring false-positive rate, end-to-end latency and processing throughput under adversarial noise. Our results show that a sequence model built on learned event Embedding improves robustness to noise while keeping latency below the interactive threshold. We release the evaluation harness and the synthetic corpus to support reproducible comparison.</source>
          <target>财务审计追踪每天新增数百万条事件，审查这些事件的团队需要这样的检测器：既能标记出真正的异常，又不会让分析师不堪重负。我们在一个合成账本事件语料库上评估三种检测方法，在对抗性噪声下测量误报率、端到端延迟和处理吞吐量。结果表明，基于学习得到的事件 Embedding 构建的序列模型可提升对噪声的健壮性，同时将延迟保持在交互阈值以下。我们公开该评估框架和合成语料库，以支持可复现的对比。</target>
