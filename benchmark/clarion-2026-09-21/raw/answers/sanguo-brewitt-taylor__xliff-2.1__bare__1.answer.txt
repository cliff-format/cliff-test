          <target state="translated">On the fifteenth day of the fourth month of the second year of Jian Ning, the emperor held court in the Wen De Hall. Just as he ascended the throne, a violent wind suddenly rose from the corner of the hall, and a great green snake flew down from the beam and coiled upon the chair. The emperor fell in fright, and his attendants hurried to carry him into the palace. All the officials fled in confusion. In a moment the snake disappeared. Then suddenly came great thunder and rain, with hail, which did not stop until midnight and destroyed countless houses. In the second month of the fourth year of Jian Ning, there was an earthquake in Luoyang. The sea overflowed, and all the coastal dwellers were swept by great waves into the sea. In the first year of Guang He, a hen turned into a cock. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor more than ten zhang long flew into the Wen De Hall. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow appeared in the Jade Hall; the cliffs of Wuyuan Mountain all split apart. These various evil omens were not merely one or two.</target>

          <target state="translated">The emperor issued an edict asking his ministers the cause of these calamities. The emperor read the memorial and sighed, then rose to change his clothes. Cao Jie secretly watched from behind and told everything to the attendants. So they framed Cai Yong on other charges and sent him back to his village.
