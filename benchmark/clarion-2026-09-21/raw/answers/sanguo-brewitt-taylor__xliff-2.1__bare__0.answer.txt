          <target>On the full-moon day of the fourth month of the second year of Jianning, the Emperor held court in Wende Hall. The Emperor fell over in fright, and his attendants hurriedly carried him into the palace. All the officials fled. In a moment the serpent disappeared. Then came great thunder and rain, with hail, which did not stop until midnight and destroyed countless houses. In the first year of Guanghe, hens turned into cocks. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor more than ten zhang long flew into Wende Hall.
