"chapter-1-014" = "Mean rooms and empty halls—in years gone by, tablets of office filled the bed; withered grass and dry willows—once a place of song and dance. Spider webs fill the carved beams; green silk is now pasted on the thatch windows. What of rouge so thick and powder so fragrant? How is it that the temples have turned to frost? Yesterday, yellow earth on the grave mound received white bones; tonight, under the red lantern, lovebirds lie in the bed-curtains. Gold fills the chest, silver fills the chest—in the twinkling of an eye, a beggar whom all revile. While sighing that another’s life is short, how do you know your own return is death? Though you teach your children well, you cannot be sure they will not later become robbers. You choose rich food and fine grain—who would expect to end up in the pleasure quarter? Because you dislike the gauze cap as too small, you bring on the cangue and chains. Yesterday you pitied a torn coat for its cold; today you dislike the purple robe for its length. In the bustling confusion, you have just finished singing and I mount the stage; you mistake a foreign land for your native place. How absurd! In the end, all is making a wedding dress for someone else! When the mad lame Taoist heard this, he clapped his hands and laughed: ‘A fitting explanation! A fitting explanation!’ Shiyin then said, ‘Let’s go!’ He snatched the bundle from the Taoist’s shoulder, carried it on his back, and without returning home, went off with the mad Taoist, floating away. At once the neighborhood was stirred, and everyone told it as a piece of news. When Mrs. Feng heard the news, she cried as if dead and came back to life, and could only consult with her father and send people everywhere to search, but what news did they find? There was no help for it; she had to rely on her parents to live. Fortunately, she still had two old maidservants to attend her. Mistress and servants, three in all, worked day and night at needlework to sell, helping her father’s expenses. Though Feng Su grumbled daily, there was nothing he could do.";
