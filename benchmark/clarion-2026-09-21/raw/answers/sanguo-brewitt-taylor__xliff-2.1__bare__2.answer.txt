          <target>On the fifteenth day of the fourth month of the second year of Jian Ning, the Emperor went up into the Wen De Hall. As he was ascending the throne, a fierce wind suddenly arose at the corner of the hall, and a huge green serpent flew down from the beam and coiled upon the throne. The Emperor was so frightened that he fell, and his attendants hurried him into the palace. All the officials fled in terror. In a moment the serpent disappeared. Then came a great thunderstorm with hail, which did not stop until midnight, destroying countless houses. In the second month of the fourth year of Jian Ning, there was an earthquake in Luoyang; the sea overflowed, and the inhabitants along the coast were swept into the sea by the great waves. In the first year of Guang He, a hen turned into a cock. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor more than ten feet long flew into the Wen De Hall. In the seventh month, a rainbow was seen in the Jade Hall; the hills and cliffs of Wuyuan all collapsed. These various portents were not confined to one kind.</target>
