        <mda:meta type="info">An original one-act scene. A closing repertory theatre on its final night. Odette Rill has run the stage for thirty years; Cassian Vale left the company a decade ago and has come back for the last performance. The entries are one continuous scene and must read in order: a line's rendering may depend on a line before it.</mda:meta>
        <mda:meta type="standard">Stage directions use a terse present-tense narrative register and never the spoken register of dialogue. Keep the venue name Halloway in Latin script. Render the surname Vale as 维尔 wherever it appears. House style: one space between Han text and Latin letters or digits, fullwidth punctuation inside Han sentences, and no textbook jargon.</mda:meta>
