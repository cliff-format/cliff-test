    <string name="chapter-1-001">On the fifteenth day of the fourth month of the second year of Jian Ning, the Emperor held court in the Wen De Hall. Just as he was mounting the throne, a violent wind suddenly rose from a corner of the hall, and a huge green serpent flew down from the beam and coiled itself upon his chair. The Emperor fell in terror; his attendants hurriedly bore him into the palace, and all the assembled officials fled for their lives. In a moment the serpent was gone. Then of a sudden came great thunder and rain, with hail, which fell until midnight and wrecked countless houses. In the second month of the fourth year of Jian Ning, Luoyang was shaken by an earthquake; again the sea overflowed, and the people along the coast were all swept into the sea by the great waves. In the first year of Guang He, a hen was changed into a cock. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor more than ten cubits long flew into the Wen De Hall. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow was seen at the Jade Hall; the hills and shores of Wuyuan all crumbled away. All these were evil omens, and not one alone.</string>
