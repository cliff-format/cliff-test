    <string name="chapter-1-001">On the fifteenth day of the fourth month of the second year of Jian Ning, the Emperor held court in the Hall of Virtue. As he was ascending the throne, a violent wind suddenly rose from a corner of the hall, and a great green serpent flew down from the beams and coiled upon the throne. The Emperor fell down in his terror; his attendants hurried him into the palace, and all the hundred officers fled and hid themselves. In a moment the serpent was gone. Then of a sudden came great thunder and rain, with hail, which did not cease till midnight, and wrecked countless houses. In the second month of the fourth year of Jian Ning, Luoyang was shaken by earthquake; again the sea overflowed its bounds, and the dwellers along the coast were all swept by the great waves into the sea. In the first year of Guang He, hens turned into cocks. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapour more than ten cubits long flew into the Hall of Virtue. In the autumn, in the seventh month, a rainbow was seen over the Jade Hall; and the cliffs of Wuyuan all crumbled and fell. Omens of evil, these, and not one alone.</string>
