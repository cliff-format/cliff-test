Spider webs fill the carved beams; green silk now pastes the rush windows. Why speak of rouge so thick and powder so fragrant, when the hair at the temples has turned to frost? Yesterday on the yellow earth mound they sent off white bones; tonight beneath the red lantern and bed curtains lie mandarin ducks. Gold fills the chests, silver fills the chests; in the blink of an eye, beggars are all reviled. While lamenting that another's life is short, how do you know your own return is death? Though taught with method, there is no assuring he will not become a robber later. In choosing a rich fare, who would expect to end up in a courtesan's lane? Because a gauze cap is too small, one brings the cangue and chains; yesterday pitying a torn coat's cold, today disliking the purple python's length. In the noisy bustle, you have just finished singing and I come on stage; instead, a foreign land is taken for home. How absurd! In the end, all is making wedding clothes for others! Hearing this, the mad lame Daoist clapped his hands and laughed: “A fitting interpretation, a fitting interpretation!” Shiyin then said, “Let's go!” He seized the bag on the Daoist's shoulder, slung it on his own back, and without going home at all, went off floating with the mad Daoist. At once the neighborhood was stirred, and everyone told it as a piece of news. When Lady Feng heard this, she cried herself half dead. She had no choice but to consult her father and send people everywhere to search, but what news could be found? There was nothing to be done; she had to rely on her parents for a living. Fortunately, two old maids were still with her to serve her. Mistress and servants, three in all, sewed day and night to sell their work, helping her father's expenses.
