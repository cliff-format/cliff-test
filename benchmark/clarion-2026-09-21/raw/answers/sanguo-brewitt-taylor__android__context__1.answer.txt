    <string name="chapter-1-001">On the fifteenth day of the fourth month of the second year of Jianning, the Emperor held court in the Hall of Virtue. Just as he ascended the throne, a violent wind suddenly rose from a corner of the hall. Then a great green serpent flew down from the beam and coiled upon the throne. The Emperor was so frightened that he fell; his attendants hurried him into the palace, and all the officials fled in panic. In a moment, the serpent vanished. Suddenly there came great thunder and rain, together with hail, which did not stop until midnight, destroying countless houses. In the second month of the fourth year of Jianning, an earthquake struck Luoyang; the sea also overflowed, and the coastal residents were all swept into the sea by the great waves. In the first year of Guanghe, a hen turned into a rooster. On the first day of the sixth month, a black vapor more than ten zhang long flew into the Hall of Virtue. In the seventh month of autumn, a rainbow appeared over the Jade Hall; the mountains and cliffs of Wuyuan all collapsed and split apart. These various ominous signs were not limited to one kind.</string>

    <string name="chapter-1-002">The Emperor issued an edict asking the ministers the cause of these calamities. The Gentleman Consultant Cai Yong submitted a memorial, saying that falling rainbows and hens turning into roosters were caused by women and eunuchs interfering in government, and his words were quite blunt and direct. The Emperor sighed when he read the memorial, then rose to change his clothes. Cao Jie secretly watched from behind and told everything to those around him.
