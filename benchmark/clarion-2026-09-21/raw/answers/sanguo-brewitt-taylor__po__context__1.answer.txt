msgstr "After a while, He Jin entered and informed the Empress Dowager, intending to put the eunuchs to death. Empress Dowager He said, “Eunuchs have commanded the palace for generations; it is the established practice of the Han. The late Emperor has but recently departed, and you wish to kill his old servants—this is not to honor the ancestral temple.” He Jin was by nature an irresolute man, and hearing the Empress Dowager’s words, he assented and withdrew. Yuan Shao met him and asked, “How stands the great matter?” He Jin said, “The Empress Dowager will not permit it; what can be done?” Shao said, “You may summon heroes and men of valor from all quarters to bring troops to the capital and put all the eunuchs to death. At this critical moment, the Empress Dowager will have no choice but to comply.” He Jin said, “This plan is excellent!” He at once sent proclamations to the various garrisons, summoning them to the capital."
