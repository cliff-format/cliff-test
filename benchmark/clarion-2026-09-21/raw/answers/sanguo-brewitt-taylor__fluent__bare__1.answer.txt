He Jin came of a family of butchers; because his younger sister had entered the palace as a Consort of the First Rank and had borne the Prince Bian, she was made Empress, and through her He Jin obtained power and high office. The Emperor also favored the Beauty Wang, who bore the Prince Xie. Empress He, in jealousy, poisoned the Beauty Wang. The Prince Xie was reared in the palace of the Empress Dowager Dong. The Empress Dowager Dong was the mother of Emperor Ling and the wife of Liu Chang, Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion. Formerly, because Emperor Huan had no son, they had brought in the son of the Marquis of Jiedu Pavilion and set him on the throne, and he became Emperor Ling. When Emperor Ling succeeded to the throne, he brought his mother to be cared for in the palace and honored her as Empress Dowager.

chapter-2-004 = The Empress Dowager Dong was delighted. The next day, when court was held, she issued an edict enfeoffing the Prince Xie as Prince of Chenliu, making Dong Zhong General of Agile Cavalry, and admitting Zhang Rang and the others to a share in the government. When the Empress Dowager He saw that the Empress Dowager Dong was monopolizing power, she gave a banquet in the palace and invited the Empress Dowager Dong to attend. When the wine had gone half round, the Empress Dowager He rose, raised her cup, bowed twice, and said: "We are both women, and it is not fitting that we meddle in the affairs of state. In former days, because Empress Lü grasped great power, a thousand members of her clan were put to the sword. Now we should dwell deep within the ninefold palace; let the great affairs of court be left to the great ministers and elders to deliberate among themselves — that would be the country's good fortune. I beg you to give ear to this." The Empress Dowager Dong flew into a rage and said: "You poisoned the Beauty Wang to death, your heart is full of jealousy. The Empress He was angered too and said: "I advised you in good faith — why do you rage instead?" chapter-2-006 = The Registrar Chen Lin said: "That will not do! As the common saying goes, 'To catch swallows and sparrows with your eyes shut' — that is self-deception. Even a small creature cannot be deceived to gain one's end, how much less the great affairs of state? Now, General, you rely on the imperial majesty and hold the keys of the army; you stride like a dragon and a tiger, and the high and the low are as you will. If you wish to slay the eunuchs, it is no more than blowing up a great furnace to singe a hair. You have only to act swiftly, exercise your authority and decide on the spot, and Heaven and man will follow you. But instead you would send proclamations out to the great ministers, so that they come pressing to the very gates of the palace; then the heroes will gather, each with his own designs. That is what is called holding the spear and halberd reversed, and handing the handle to another: the deed would never be accomplished, but disorder would arise instead." He Jin laughed and said: "That is the view of a coward!" A man beside him clapped his hands and laughed aloud: "This matter is as easy as turning one's palm — why argue so much?"
